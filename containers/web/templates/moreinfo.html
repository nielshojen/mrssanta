Application trust on your Mac

Santa checks apps before they run, helps you make a safe choice, and keeps {{ response.organization }} devices protected.

What Santa does

Apps that are already known are allowed automatically. Unknown apps can be reviewed and allowed when they are not considered malicious.

If an app is approved on {{ response.vote_threshold }} unique Macs, the rule can become global and the app will be allowed on all Macs.

Step 1

Review the prompt

When Santa blocks an unknown app, confirm that it is the app you intended to open.

Step 2

Check the scan result

If the security scan passes, the app can be allowed for your Mac.

Step 3

Allow the app

After allowing it, your Mac will receive the rule after the next Santa sync.

Step 4

Sync faster if needed

Use the Santa menu bar icon and choose Sync to request a rule update sooner.

Managed apps

Managed apps, including apps that require approval or have a license cost, are blocked until installed by {{ response.selfserviceapp }}.

Blocked apps

Apps that are not approved or are considered malicious are blocked from running.
